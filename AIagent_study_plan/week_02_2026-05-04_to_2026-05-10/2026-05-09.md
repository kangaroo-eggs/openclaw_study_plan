**Review words used:** coral, cape, irritate, purify, examinee, firecracker, flaw, definitive, exclusion, porter

A loud **firecracker** from the festival began to **irritate** nervous students, and one **porter** found a serious **flaw** in the seating list: a wheelchair user had been left out by **exclusion**. The coordinator made a **definitive** change, moved the desk beside a **coral** mural, and the exam finally began fairly.

**中文：** 在海角附近的海边考场，每位考生都拿到了志愿者用便携滤器净化过的水。节庆传来的爆竹声开始刺激紧张的学生，一名搬运工发现座位表有严重缺陷：一位使用轮椅的人被排除在外。协调员立刻作出明确决定，把桌子移到一幅珊瑚壁画旁，考试终于公平地开始了。
