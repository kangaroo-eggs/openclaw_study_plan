**Review words used:** inventory, pilgrim, foe, illuminate, exempt, certify, feasible, interpreter, computerize, cashier

**English:** At the old temple shop, Mei helped a tired **pilgrim** find medicine while the **cashier** counted the nightly **inventory**. A power cut made the shelves dark, so Mei used a lantern to **illuminate** labels and asked an **interpreter** to explain prices to visitors.
